# Lesson 3: Draft

**Goal:** Turn a specific topic into text the human can review.

Call `draft_post` with:

- `topic`: one concrete idea;
- `tone`: `clear`, `warm`, or `technical`;
- `cta`: optional short call to action.

The output includes `character_count` and `within_x_limit`.

The starter uses a deterministic template. Its output is not model-generated.
The purpose is to show the contract between an agent and a content adapter, not
to judge writing quality.

Review questions:

1. Does the opening make one concrete promise?
2. Does the body contain a real observation?
3. Is the call to action specific?
4. Are there claims that need a source?
