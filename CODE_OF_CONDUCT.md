# Code of Conduct

Contributors must keep project discussions respectful, specific, and focused
on improving the software. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing,
credential sharing, and attempts to expose another person's private data are
not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when conduct creates
an unsafe or unproductive project environment. Report conduct concerns through
the private channel in `SECURITY.md` rather than publishing private details.
